My dog has been weeing in the house recently. The kitchen is the area he tends to wee most often. My proposal is to set up a Raspberry Pi and camera in the kitchen. This is to detect each time the dog wees. Upon weeing, SenseHat should use blinking red LED lights and an annoying noise to discourage bad behaviour. An MQTT broker (e.g. Mosquito) will be used to send structured JSON information. Upon trigger/detection, an alert will be sent to a Blynk web/mobile app providing live updates. Dashboards will be set up on the Blynk web/mobile app to monitor times of day and frequency of dog weeing each day. This will be used to detect consistent patterns and any potential changes over time. Averages will be determined based on historic data.

The main difficulties will be getting the Raspberry Pi, camera and SenseHat to determine first that it is the dog in the kitchen as opposed to myself or my grandmother. I will use an open source identifier to distinguish between dogs vs humans. I've considered YOLO for this, but I will explore other potential options. Due to the limited timeframe, training the model detection myself probably isn't possible, so I am trying to find a pre-trained open source model to identify a dog urinating. I think I'll be able to find a pre-trained model to at least identify that it is a dog as opposed to a human. After the dog species has been identified, the next difficult step will be identifying a reliable movement pattern to detect when the dog is weeing. I have not found a pre-trained model that looks reliable yet, and I am still unsure how to potentially implement this at this stage. Potential behaviour to establish a pattern could be: -dog becomes stationary (relatively) -remains in the same spot for several seconds -if possible, detecting a shift of body and lifting of the leg would be ideal, but this may prove too specific to actually detect.

I will configure thresholds and cooldowns to ensure the Raspberry Pi doesn't detect the dog weeing and then spam notifications due to interpreting one wee as multiple wees for example. Lighting could also be a difficulty (light on vs off). The positioning and range of the camera's perception is also a potential difficulty, though I have an ideal position in the kitchen in mind for the Raspberry Pi.

Potential technologies to consider implementing - SQL, MongoDB, Docker, a cloud platform (AWS/Azure/GCP). Docker to help with separation of concerns (this is also a technology I wanted to learn how to use). A backend API could ingest the data into a cloud database. I might consider making thresholds editable via Blynk's UI, which then communicates directly with the Python code.
